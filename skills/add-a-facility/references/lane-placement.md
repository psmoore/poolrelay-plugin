# Placing programs on lanes when the source doesn't say which

Place programs the way a pool deck actually runs, then say the placement is an estimate.

## The layout

- **Lap swim is anchored at lane 1.** It takes the run of free lanes from lane 1 upward.
- **Everything else packs in from the far end** as side-by-side blocks. The longest-running program sits
  outermost (for example open swim all afternoon, or a club practice). Short classes and lessons sit inward,
  next to lap swim, so that when a class ends its lanes rejoin lap swim without a gap.

## Typical sizes

| Program | Lanes |
|---|---|
| Class, water fitness, lesson block | 2 lanes (1 in a pool of 4 or fewer) |
| Swim team or club, sharing with lap swim | Half the pool |
| Swim team or club, pool closed to the public | Every lane not used by a class |
| Open or family swim, sharing with lap swim | 3 lanes (2 in a pool of 6 or fewer) |
| Open or family swim, alone | Every free lane |
| Meet, closure, all-day event | Every lane |

Use the source's own numbers whenever it gives them: "2 lanes only", "limited lanes", "4 lanes 50m".

## Split when lanes change

One event holds one set of lanes for its whole time. When lap swim gives up lanes 5–6 for a 9am class and
takes them back at 10am, that is three events:

- lap swim 6–9am, lanes 1–6
- lap swim 9–10am, lanes 1–4
- lap swim 10am–noon, lanes 1–6

Don't keep one long event and let the class sit on its lanes.

Split by weekday as well: lap swim on Monday/Wednesday and lap swim on Tuesday/Thursday are separate series
when their lane counts differ. Merge weekdays that come out identical.

Gaps shorter than about 30 minutes between classes aren't worth a separate event. Fold them into the
neighboring event, keeping only the lanes free in both.

## 50 m pools

Book each event on the lanes of the configuration it swims. A block marked "long course", "LC", "50m" or
"LCM" goes on the Long Course lanes. A lane count larger than the long-course total means short course. In a
season when the pool runs short course all day, everything goes on the Short Course lanes.
