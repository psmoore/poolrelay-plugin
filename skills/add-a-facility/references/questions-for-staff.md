# Questions for the pool staff

A facility's own pages rarely agree with each other. Every disagreement, gap and estimate is a question for
the staff. These questions are also the most useful thing to bring to a first conversation with the facility.

## What to look for

| Kind | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Conflict** | The Hours page says lap swim until 7:30pm, but the schedule closes the pool at 3pm for high school practice. The home page says open swim starts at 6:30; the Hours page says 6:45. Registration names a class "(5:00 PM)" but it meets 6–7pm. |
| **Stale** | The lesson list is from a session that ended weeks ago. A club's practice page shows last year's schedule. |
| **Gap** | A program has a page but no times. A team's practice times are behind an interest form. A season's dates are "TBA" while registration already lists the classes. |
| **Copied** | Two facilities' schedules are identical minute for minute. Ask whether the text was copied or the hours really match. |
| **Real overlap** | Two classes are listed in the same water at the same time. Ask how they share it. |
| **Estimate** | A lane count or lane placement you had to estimate. |

## How to write them

One line each: the kind of problem, what each source says with its exact times, and the question. For example:

> **Conflict.** North Campus lap swim: the Hours page says until 7:30pm, and the pool schedule closes the pool 3–9pm Tuesday and Thursday for high school meets. Which is right on meet days?
